01 / PROBLEM
The Challenge

Problem & Dataset

Motion-controlled gaming requires specialized hardware. Can a standard webcam replace all of that?

  • 6-class temporal classification — idle, jab, cross, hook, uppercut, block_high
  • 2-second sliding window — classify from 60 frames of body landmarks
  • Hard latency constraint — <33 ms to keep pace with 30 fps webcam
  • No specialized hardware — standard 720p laptop webcam only

Each sample: (60, 132) array — 60 frames × 33 landmarks × 4 values. Hip-centered, torso-scaled normalization. Stratified 60/20/20 split.

03 / MODELS
Three Models Evaluated

Naive Baseline · Classical ML · Deep Learning

Nearest Centroid

Per-class mean vector (7920-dim). Predict closest centroid. No feature engineering.

50.0%

<1 ms  ·  3× above chance

Random Forest

200 trees, max_depth=20. Trained on 396 temporal stats. StandardScaler preprocessing.

97.1%

~3 ms  ·  Deployed

2-Layer LSTM

hidden=64, dropout=0.3. Raw (60×132) input. Early stopping patience=50. PyTorch.

92.9%

~15 ms  ·  ~8 min train

Why RF beats LSTM: With ~600 samples, hand-crafted temporal statistics provide stronger inductive bias than end-to-end sequence learning. The LSTM must learn from scratch what std/range/delta encode explicitly — more data would close the gap.
